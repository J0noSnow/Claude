Shared principles, used in every draft below:
1. The first line is a complete, quotable claim. The feed often shows only that line.
2. One idea per post, usually under 70 words on Substack and under 280 characters on X.
3. Second person for the claim, one real detail from your life for the proof.
4. Concrete nouns and ordered rules beat abstractions.
5. Name the cost or the pattern out loud. Your own X notes found that pure encouragement is saturated and the cost-then-reversal shape is what travels now.
6. End on a contrast or a door left open. No link, no pitch.
