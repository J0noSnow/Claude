# 5 notes, "October is almost over" angle

Essay: "The phrase multi-passionate people use to shrink themselves"
Angle: October has 10 days left (Oct 22 to 31), and the essay has exactly 10 prompts left in that window (Week 4 has 7, the last three days have 3).
Hannah Bay's structure kept: a feeling-about-the-moment hook, a concrete number, a soft ask, reply bait. Her phrasing is not reused.

---

## 1. The write-off
**Pattern:** feeling-as-fact hook + the number + a soft ask + reply bait

> October is almost over, which is usually when we write the month off.
>
> There are 10 days left and 10 prompts left in this month's journal. One a day, about ten minutes, all aimed at the phrase multi-passionate people use to shrink themselves.
>
> Ten days is plenty to change one sentence you've been saying about yourself for years. It's here if you want something to do with them besides scroll. Day 1 is 👀

## 2. The piano sentence
**Pattern:** confession that creates a gap + reply bait

> I know one song on piano, played slowly. I will never call myself a pianist.
>
> October has 10 days left, which is enough time to change what I say about it. There's a phrase I reach for every time, and this month's prompts are about catching it.
>
> What do you say when someone asks what you do? Tell me below.

## 3. Three questions before it ends
**Pattern:** the value is in the note, the essay holds the rest

> 3 questions to ask yourself before October ends:
>
> 1. Which way is your energy leaning this season, and what drains it fastest?
> 2. How do you choose a direction without choosing one thing?
> 3. What would you call yourself if no one could ask a follow-up question?
>
> The other 7 for the last 10 days are in this month's essay.

## 4. You're not behind
**Pattern:** a clear structure and a low barrier to start

> Missed the first three weeks of October's prompts? You're not behind.
>
> Weeks 1 to 3 are for hearing the phrase, what it protects, and the skill underneath. The last 10 days are where you decide which way you're leaning and say it differently.
>
> No catching up. Skip back whenever you want. Ten minutes and a pen.
>
> October's essay is here.

## 5. The rebrand deadline
**Pattern:** behind-the-scenes note + the essay's announcements

> If I got a dollar every time I rebranded myself, I'd be a hundredaire.
>
> So I'm locking it in with 10 days of October to spare: Jono's Journal. Permanent, unless I change my mind 🙃. Everything I make starts in the journal anyway.
>
> There's also a chat now for people who refuse to pick one thing. Details and the rest of October's prompts are in this month's essay.

## Notes
- **The count.** "10 days" is right if you post on Oct 22. Change the number to the days left, and the prompt count with it. Week 4 (Oct 22 to 28) has 7 prompts and Oct 29 to 31 has 3, so on Oct 25 it's 7 days and 7 prompts.
- **"Change your life" is too big.** The essay can't back that up in 10 days. I used "change one sentence you've been saying about yourself", which it can.
- **Post 3** uses prompts word for word from the essay: two from Week 4 and one from the last three days.
- **Essay card.** Attach the essay as a restack card on each note, as Hannah does.
