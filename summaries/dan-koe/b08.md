## 1. "How smart creators will make money in 2026" (2025-05-22)
Gist: Courses are static; the next product is education plus AI prompts that execute using the buyer's own context.
- [C] OPINION: info keeps its value because it is labor; people who won't pay won't pay attention. Courses fail because buyers can't adapt a static lesson to their situation.
- [C] OPINION: the three offer types are DIY / done-with-you / done-for-you; AI can collapse them into one product (education + execution).
- [C] DEMO: 4-step build: (1) write your process as instructions for an employee, which doubles as curriculum outline; (2) turn each step into a prompt with two phases (interview for context, then execute); (3) use it yourself, iterate until output is 80-90% as good as you; (4) write documentation teaching both the task and the prompts.
- [C] DEMO shortcut: buy a successful competitor product, have AI dissect its structure, then reuse that as a template for your own.
- [L] DEMO: announces a 30-day personal-brand challenge as a test of the theory before building it into his software; early-bird discount, date given; ends with links to paid posts (digital product mega-guide, sales page guide, "launch strategy next") = paid-post ladder.
- [I] Frame: "static info vs. a piece of the creator's mind that acts"; names "mind, model, methods."
- Exercise: write step-by-step instructions for one task you do in your writing process, as if to a new hire.
Form: claim-stacked opening ("I have a prediction"), 3 numbered beliefs, scenario/imagine section, numbered how-to, soft CTA list at the end. ~2,500 words. Heavy tool plugs (own software). Sign-off "Thanks for reading. - Dan".

## 2. "You can just make more money" (2025-07-28)
Gist: Money problems are really belief problems; wealth scales with tolerated uncertainty.
- [I] Hook pattern: "There are 2 types of people who will read this" - splits readers by reaction to a provocative title, flatters the second type.
- [I] Argument pattern: reframe ("Money problems don't exist. Psychological problems do"), list of common limiting beliefs, then a tiered ladder (employee -> freelancer -> info seller -> exec -> investor) to show a cap at each level.
- [I] Coined formula: potential = uncertainty embraced x time you can endure it.
- [L] OPINION/DEMO: choose any no-capital model (info, coaching, freelance, agency); the real two levers are capturing attention (writing, social, storytelling) and converting it (sales, marketing, offers). A year of trial and error gives "buttons you can press."
- [A] Lesson: learn attention by doing; start one channel and iterate.
- DEMO (personal): "strategic poverty" - 8 housemates, $300-400 rent, ~$50/wk food, min-wage part-time job while testing models; tried dropshipping, agencies, freelancing.
- Practice metaphor: "emotional one-rep max" built by progressive overload of responsibility.
- Exercise: list your money beliefs, then take a walk and brain-dump what you'd downsize to afford more risk.
Form: short (~2,000 words), manifesto tone, three bold numbered tactics, ends with a reflective action ("go on a walk"). Minimal CTA, just "read the last letter."

## 3. "The most profitable skill of the 21st century (not AI)" (2026-07-03)
Gist: The meta-skill is understanding human nature, taught as 3 tensions and 5 psychological levers for writing/selling.
- [I]/[N] Opens by naming a problem ("Nobody knows what skill to learn") then offers the answer; he explicitly points out he opens every letter this way. Teaches the pattern in the letter itself (self-demonstrating).
- [I] Frameworks: Three Tensions (survival, identity, progress), mapped to a Maslow-like ladder; "90% aren't ready for deep ideas, work them up the ladder."
- [A][N] 5 levers (each pairs to a tension): 1 name the threat (Schwartz 5 awareness levels); 2 mirror identity (open with "If you're..."); 3 exclude people ("this isn't for..."); 4 paint the transformation (imagination fires same circuitry as experience); 5 give a tiny first step (Zeigarnik effect).
- DEMO: cites a 3.9M-view YouTube title as packaging problem+identity+solution in 8 words; says his own high-performing social posts used survival tension.
- OPINION: you only learn this by publishing and watching response ("market is the mentor").
- [L] Plugs a writing bootcamp twice mid-letter and in P.S. ("starts in 5 days") with a deadline; also turned the whole letter into a reusable prompt for sale/use (in his software).
- Exercise: write down 5 problems you've lived = 5 content ideas; write 4 openers starting "If you're...".
Form: problem hook, thesis, headed sections, bold-numbered levers with example lines, mid-letter "subtle reminder" CTA, short sign-off, P.S. CTA. ~2,400 words.
