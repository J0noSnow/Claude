## The Greatest Thought Partner Prompt (2025-07-20) [I][N]
- Use: before writing, you give the AI a topic and it interviews you one question at a time until you hit several breakthroughs, then it assembles them into a structured narrative usable as a newsletter outline, video script outline, or several short posts. Koe says he runs it before anything he writes (DEMO claim). Premise: AI should sharpen your thinking, not replace it.
- Four drivers the prompt uses: pattern spotting (where your approach differs from the standard), paradox hunting (cases where doing the opposite works better), naming the unnamed (give a memorable label to a process you already use), contrast creation (your method vs. the common opposite).
- Rules baked in: one question at a time; challenge generic claims until specific; no compliments; don't leave a concept until it has a name; avoid bland business words; stop when there's enough material.
- Output per insight: the breakthrough, a short explanation, how it changes your world, your own key phrasing, an outside reframe, a distinctive name. Examples of the kind of insight/name it hunts: paradoxes (weakness as strength), novel concepts, contrasts between two types (e.g., creators who chase cash vs. content), heuristics, and reframes.
- Takeaway for Jono: coining named concepts and paradoxes is a core articulation technique of his.

## Prompt: Generate 50+ content ideas (2025-05-27) [I][A]
- Not meant to write for you; meant to flood your head with candidate ideas across fixed idea types: audience pain points, desires, personal stories, harsh truths, novel perspectives, beginner advice, list ideas, hooks. Feed it topic and audience. Reader comment (by Jono's Journal's account) says it helps with consistency.

## Prompt: ConsciousnessAI, 90-day plan (2025-05-16) [I]

Purpose: turn abstract self-development theory into practice. Mostly personal development, low relevance to audience building, but shows his pattern of packaging an idea as a usable tool.

Echoes his own routine of listing 2-3 priority tasks before deep work (DEMO).
