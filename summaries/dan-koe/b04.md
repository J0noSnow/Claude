## 1. The Deep Work Routine That Changed My Life (2025-05-08, Substack, free)
Gist: A minimalist daily focus routine built on "entropy" (mind drifts to disorder unless you spend effort) and games-as-quests.
- [I] OPINION: Attention as RAM; ~50 bits/sec conscious bandwidth, so lifetime "budget" is finite. Numeric/physics-flavoured metaphor makes an obvious point feel fresh. Borrows Csikszentmihalyi (boredom = challenge too low, anxiety = too high).
- [A][N] DEMO: Quantify the task ("write 1,000 words," not "work on newsletter"); give it a 60-min block so there is no time to deliberate; unfinished work (Zeigarnik effect) makes the next block easier to start.
- [A] DEMO: Structure: 2-3 most-important tasks across 2-3 blocks; deep work first thing (low entropy, no email/phone); shallow work (email, posting-and-leaving) only afterward.
- [I] DEMO: Second half of day is "leisure maxxing" (walks, books, smart people) because his output depends on ideas; "the people who work least may win because story and synthesis are the moat vs AI."
- [L] Plugs: free Kortex workflow, a paid post (AI prompt for deep-work sessions), a next paid post on building one high-quality piece into a week of content ("build a world" vs sleazy funnel). Soft CTA footer lists 3 offers.
- Exercise: Tomorrow, write one quantified output target and time-box it to 60 minutes before opening email.
Form: Staccato one-line opening ("1 hour, 1 project, 1 vision"), then metaphor, numbered points (1-3), short sentences, ~1,900 words. Apology-for-simplicity move ("forgive me, nothing revolutionary") lowers defences. Ends "Thank you for reading. - Dan" then offer list.

# Cross-letter patterns for the playbook
- Recurring concept bank: attention/focus, entropy, flow (challenge vs skill), survival-of-identity (memes), vision + anti-vision, unknown, "transcend and include," you-are-the-niche. Almost every letter reuses 3-4 of them, so the voice is consistent and each letter feels like part of one worldview.
- Argument shape: relatable pain -> reframe -> named framework with numbered parts -> personal anecdote -> action list -> ominous or paradoxical closer. Sign-off "- Dan"; word counts 1,200-5,000, newer ones shorter and with sharper hooks.
- Monetisation form: free letter does the thinking; offers sit in footers and mid-letter reminders, with a ladder of $5 community -> low-price courses -> cohort/masterclass -> software. Rarely hard-sells inside body, except the product letter.
- Content engine (DEMO): write one newsletter, derive 3 posts/day and a thread/week, cross-post, repurpose to video/blog; rewrite best-performers monthly.
