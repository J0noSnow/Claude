## 3. How to become so creative it feels illegal (2026-03-24, Substack, free)

- [N] DEMO: has written a newsletter almost every week for 5 years, hit a slump; shows how he deals with creative drought.
- [L][C] Mid-letter blockquote pitch for a paid challenge ("Build a 2-Hour Content System in 14 Days", 14 prompts, early-bird pricing ends in 3 days); a sponsor-ish nootropics plug; both marked as asides in blockquotes.

## 4. Full Course: How To Turn Your Interests Into Profitable a Lifestyle Business (2026-10-05, Substack, free)
Gist: step-by-step blueprint: content map, tool stack, micro product + micro service, getting first client; "soft rich".

Idea types: pain points, how-tos, lessons learned, why it matters, harsh truths, opinions on popular ideas. "Posts are puzzle pieces, not darts." (OPINION framed as steps.)
- [A] Broad topics, narrow later via offers; "one or two steps ahead" is enough; being a learner in public is relatable.
- [N] Pair one short-form platform with one long-form (Substack as both; his pick). Newsletter = where you demonstrate competence and make most money. Put the offer CTA mid and end of every newsletter.
- [L] DEMO/OPINION pricing: micro product ~$19 (template/guide from a validated newsletter); micro service = 4 coaching calls at $500-$1,000 (5 sales = $5k month); can upsell to 4 more calls ($2,000). Promote once a day.
- [L] Validated angle: find posts/videos that already went viral on your topic and name the product after that angle (e.g. "how to focus 12 hours" -> study product). Gives AI prompts (Eden/Claude) for angle research, product brainstorm, and micro-service outline (interview one question at a time).
- [C] Micro-service outline: your story, where they are now, 1-month outcome, dump every step then group into 4 calls, a name (systems sell), homework, onboarding questions; call structure: last week's tasks / pain points / next steps. "Coaching notes become your course." Tutorial route (teach not do) vs coaching route.

No sales page. DM script: ask goal, give one expert tip, soft promote, follow up in 2-3 days with value, one more follow-up then let go. Math: 1-5k impressions, 30-40 questionnaires, 20-30 DMs -> 5 buyers; 1-2 hrs/day.
- Heavy product placement for his own app, Eden (store/workspace); treat as company marketing.
Exercises: draw the content map on paper; write Point A/B in one sentence each; draft a 4-call outline; write a 5-question application form.
Form: personal-story hook against "niche down", promise ("more useful than 99% of business content"), "Step 1-4" H2s with H3 substeps, templates in blockquotes, copy-paste prompts, a math section as proof. ~7,000 words. Free on Substack despite "Full Course" title; CTA is embedded product use rather than an ask.

## 7. How To Make Money As A Content Creator In 2024 (The Build Teach Earn Method) (undated, Nov 2023, Black Friday banner)
Gist: how a beginner earns via content: five writing ingredients and a Build -> Teach -> Earn path.
- [A] Content = how you attract people to a product. Five ingredients: (1) relatable broad topics (broad on top of funnel, narrow at product page); (2) impactful ideas: pick a side, aim for "90% liked / 10% disliked", firm beliefs held loosely; (3) novel perspectives from books/personal experience; (4) structure: line breaks, study high-performing structures, lists (bullets open curiosity loops), hook > body > conclusion; (5) effort: reply to bigger accounts, network. Algorithm is human psychology: desired outcome, burning problem, clear solution (OPINION).
- [A] DEMO: wrote a list post from a Medium headline on the spot (shows idea-to-post speed).
- [L][C] Build Teach Earn: build to learn skills (designing your own profile, writing your own landing pages); teach publicly (struggling to teach exposes gaps); earn once you have 5-10k followers by selling a technique not a skill; productize results with a named "unique mechanism" plus vision/anti-vision on landing page.
- [C] DEMO: turned a thread on repurposing writing into the 2-Hour Writer course; bought 5-10 web design/freelancing courses before making his own ("sell what's already selling"); made a planner because he used planners. Launch a product or service every quarter for 2-3 years (OPINION/DEMO).
- [L] Funnel: top (social) / middle (newsletter, lead magnet, DMs) / bottom (products). Also: "monetize early" stance.
- Stat quoted: creator economy $250B to $480B projection.
Exercise: take a headline from another field and write a list post in 20 minutes; list the products you've bought and use as product ideas.
Form: sweeping macro-trend opening, definition of "content" as structured information, H2 big claims, ends "Until next week, Dan". ~4,000 words, with a mid-letter promo banner.
