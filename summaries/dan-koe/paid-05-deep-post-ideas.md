# Prompt: How I generate deep post ideas with AI (2025-05-06) [I][A]
Own-words summary. All 16 paid letters have now been supplied by the user.
- Koe says AI still can't write social posts that feel right, and he doesn't want it to; he uses AI only to produce idea scaffolds.
- Method: feed a source (his own past writing, a video transcript, or a book) and ask for post ideas from it. The prompt extracts idea types he finds easiest to turn into posts: paradoxes, quotes, big ideas, pain points, aspirational statements, action steps, objections, plus a transformation arc and examples, so each "post outline" is cohesive.
- He calls the richer outlines "micro articles"; they can also become full newsletters.
- Takeaway: build a repeatable extraction step (source -> idea types -> outline) and write the final words yourself. A commenter's test of AI turning such an outline into a full article produced something that still sounded like generic AI writing, which supports his point.
