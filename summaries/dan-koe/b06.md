## 1. The Most Profitable Niche In The Creator Economy Right Now (2025-05-15)
Gist: Walks back/clarifies his old "you are the niche" line; proposes "mission-based" positioning over topic-based niching.
**Lessons**
- [A][I] OPINION: Topic-based niche (audience+topic) boxes you in, rewards niche-hopping, punishes multi-interest people. Mission-based = brand around a transformation/throughline, so any idea that moves people toward it is fair game. Both work; choice is personality-dependent.
- [A] OPINION: Social content is shown to random people (not targeted like ads), so it must be desirable enough to follow. People follow leaders; leaders have a mission.
- [I] OPINION: Idea generation gets easier with a mission; any read/watch/listen item can be filtered through it and respun. DEMO: after rebranding his newsletter to "future/proof" his ideas "flooded back" after a stale stretch; earlier mission "Modern Mastery" produced his best work.
- [I][A] OPINION: Conviction beats nuance. Over-hedging kills action; slight exaggeration gets the point across; a wrong bold idea teaches you more than being a leaf in the wind.
- [I] Brand-building 4 steps (OPINION, framework): (1) an enemy to attack (a way of life you despise; he used the 9-5 zombie life), (2) an identity for followers to adopt (name the mission as who they become: Future Proof, 2-Hour Writer, Modern Mastery), brainstorm 5-10 names and sleep on them, (3) build a "world" to explore (body of work bingeable in a week; MCU analogy: movies=long-form, spin-offs=short-form, action figures=products), (4) 80% why / 20% how.
- [A] DEMO: posted 30-40 YouTube videos before one hit; earlier ones at 2-3k views then rose past 100k after a hit. Lesson: old posts get discovered at growth spurt; 10 posts and quitting means nothing.
- [A] OPINION tactic list: find 3-5 accounts on similar themes, sort by popular, note 5-10 best pieces, recreate topics from your angle; teach fundamentals repeatedly because followers are beginners; read non-mainstream books and spin their ideas.
- [A][N] 80/20: short-form lives on "why should they care" (pain point, benefit to average person), not on how-to value; keep basics for a later beginner product. Pre-send checklist: why care? what pain solved? how does it help the average person?
- [L] DEMO: web design was the product but only ~5% of content was about it; he grew by selling the "why" of web design.
**Exercises**: Write answers to enemy / identity / name list; list 3-5 reference accounts and their top 5 pieces and draft your angle on each.
**Form**: Hook = bold claim + retraction of a past slogan; conflict-driven ("end this debate"). Headers numbered 1-4, short paragraphs, bullet-heavy, 2 big sections. ~1,600 words. Mid-letter subscribe plug ("twice weekly"); end CTAs stack: paid post, product (Kortex), Saturday weekly digest list (175k+). Sign-off "– Dan".
